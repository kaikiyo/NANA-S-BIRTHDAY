NANA'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
Open index.html in a browser to preview.

Included:
- Identity check: YES enters, NO reveals the supplied angry-cat image.
- Supplied photo on the identity page.
- Your shared picture as the birthday-page background.
- English and Japanese birthday letter.
- Audio extracted from your supplied screen recording, with play/pause.
- Interactive birthday cake: click to blow out the candle and reveal the wish.

To publish:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in a browser.
3. Drag the extracted nana_birthday_website folder onto the page.
4. Netlify will publish it and give you a shareable URL.
